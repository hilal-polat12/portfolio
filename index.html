Aircraft Parts

Personal engineering study

Airplane wing cover system

Conceptual multi-panel retractable wing flap cover system, studied across 4 aircraft classes (Cessna 172S, ATR 72-600, 737-800, 777-300ER). Full aerodynamic, structural/certification-load, and safety (FMEA) analysis, plus conceptual CAD for all 4 aircraft. Net fuel/economic benefit was positive only for the 737-800, showing the system is aircraft-class-specific rather than universal. 4 of 10 development stages complete (through Structural Load/Certification, with a conceptual preview of Detailed CAD).
