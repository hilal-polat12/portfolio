About

Hilal Polat is a Mechanical & Industrial & 3D Designer based in Türkiye, working at the intersection of classical industrial design and applied mechanical engineering. The practice runs along two parallel tracks: functional mechanical and industrial parts, including aircraft flap and cover systems, automotive components, and machine parts, engineered with attention to load paths, tolerancing, and certification-grade documentation; and sculptural 3D-print design, where each home-decoration, miniature, and tabletop piece is developed as a full print-ready brief covering wall thickness, layer height, support strategy, and material selection, rather than a rendered concept alone.

CAD work runs through SolidWorks and Fusion 360 for parametric, tolerance-driven modeling of load-bearing geometry, while Blender, ZBrush, and Cinema 4D handle the organic, sculptural, and rendering side of the print catalogue. Every project, whether an aircraft panel or a candle holder, is treated as an engineering problem first: the geometry has to make physical and structural sense before it becomes a finished drawing or a printable file.

Aircraft Parts

Personal engineering study

Airplane wing cover system

Conceptual multi-panel retractable wing flap cover system, studied across 4 aircraft classes (Cessna 172S, ATR 72-600, 737-800, 777-300ER). Full aerodynamic, structural/certification-load, and safety (FMEA) analysis, plus conceptual CAD for all 4 aircraft. Net fuel/economic benefit was positive only for the 737-800, showing the system is aircraft-class-specific rather than universal. 4 of 10 development stages complete (through Structural Load/Certification, with a conceptual preview of Detailed CAD).
